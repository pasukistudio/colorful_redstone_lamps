# 🌈 Colorful Redstone Lamps

Tired of the same old orange Redstone Lamp?

**Colorful Redstone Lamps** adds vibrant Redstone Lamps in **every vanilla dye color** — plus fully functional **Inverted variants** and matching **Panels, Slabs and Stairs** for advanced Redstone setups and detailed builds.

More color.  
More control.  
More creative freedom.

***

## ✨ Features

✔ Redstone Lamps in all vanilla dye colors  
✔ Fully functional **Inverted Redstone Lamps**  
✔ **Lamp Panels, Slabs and Stairs** in every color  
✔ Survival-friendly crafting  
✔ 100% Redstone compatible  
✔ Seamless vanilla integration

No unnecessary complexity.  
Just clean, colorful Redstone lighting — in every shape you need.

***

## 🔄 Inverted Lamps

The inverted variants behave like classic inverted Redstone Lamps:

*   💡 On when unpowered
*   🔌 Off when powered
*   Perfect for compact Redstone builds
*   Ideal for technical and decorative setups

Build smarter circuits without sacrificing aesthetics.

***

## 🧱 Panels, Slabs and Stairs

Every lamp color (normal and inverted) comes in three additional shapes:

*   **Panels** — thin, 2-pixel-deep lamps that can be placed on walls, floors and ceilings
*   **Slabs** — bottom, top and double, just like vanilla slabs
*   **Stairs** — all corner shapes, placeable in every direction

All shapes react to Redstone exactly like the full lamp block, including the inverted behavior.

***

## 🛠 Crafting

| Result | Recipe |
| --- | --- |
| Colored Lamp | Redstone Lamp + Dye |
| Inverted Lamp | Colored Lamp (shapeless, works both ways) |
| 12 Panels | 2 Lamps side by side |
| Lamp | 6 Panels (3 × 2) |
| 6 Slabs | 3 Lamps in a row |
| 4 Stairs | 6 Lamps in a stair pattern |

Panels, Slabs and Stairs can also be switched between normal and inverted by crafting them on their own.
